NLP Text Preprocessing Pipeline

Multi-stage sequence transformation pipeline from raw natural language strings to padded numerical tensor representations.

6-Stage End-to-End NLP Transformation Flow
Step 1
Text Cleaning

Lowercase, strip HTML, expand contractions, retain negations.

Step 2
Tokenization

Split sentences into discrete lexical word tokens.

Step 3
Vocabulary Index

Top 10,000 words fitted strictly on training set (No Leakage).

Step 4
Integer Encoding

Map tokens to IDs. Out-of-vocabulary mapped to <OOV> (Index 1).

Step 5
Sequence Padding

Post-pad with 0 to length 250 (options 50, 100, 250, 500).

Step 6
Batch Tensors

(batch_size, 250) fed identically to Simple RNN and LSTM.

Critical Academic Design Rule: Preserving Negations

In standard NLP text preprocessing, naive stopword removal libraries (e.g. default NLTK lists) blindly purge words such as "not", "no", "never", and "neither". In sentiment analysis, stripping negation words causes catastrophic semantic inversion:

Original Sentence Naive Preprocessing (Flawed) SentimentAI Pipeline (Correct)
"The acting was not good." "acting good" → Predicts Positive (Inverted!) "acting not good" → Predicts Negative (Preserved)
"I could never recommend this movie." "could recommend movie" → Predicts Positive (Inverted!) "could never recommend movie" → Predicts Negative (Preserved)
