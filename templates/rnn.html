Recurrent Mathematical Formulation:

h_t = tanh(W_xh • x_t + W_hh • h_{t-1} + b_h)

Because \( W_{hh} \) is multiplied recursively \( T \) times during Backpropagation Through Time (BPTT), gradients scale as \( (W_{hh})^T \), triggering exponential gradient decay for distant word dependencies.
